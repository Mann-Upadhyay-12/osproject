# Convoy effect: one long CPU job arrives just before four short ones.
# FCFS makes every short job wait behind "Long" (high average wait).
# Non-preemptive SJF is no better: Long is alone at t=0, so it starts and
# cannot be interrupted. SRTF / RR / MLFQ let the short jobs through.
cores 1
frames 8
disk_latency 2
sched fcfs
repl lru
quantum 3

proc Long arrival=0 bursts=C24
proc S1 arrival=1 bursts=C3
proc S2 arrival=2 bursts=C3
proc S3 arrival=3 bursts=C2
proc S4 arrival=4 bursts=C4
